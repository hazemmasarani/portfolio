Scaling State-Space Models on Multiple GPUs with Tensor Parallelism

Selective state-space models are promising for long-context language modeling, but single-GPU memory, bandwidth, and latency become deployment bottlenecks. Our work presents a communication-efficient tensor-parallel design that keeps recurrent updates local, caches SSM state across prefill and decode, and uses quantized AllReduce to reduce synchronization cost.
